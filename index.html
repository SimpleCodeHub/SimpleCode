How we work — zero delays, zero errors.

Our team operates with flawless precision — there's no room for delays or mistakes. Every part of the process is handled by a dedicated specialist, from sales to coordinators, developers, and managers.

01 // SALES

Sales team

They receive your request, understand your needs precisely, and recommend the right plan and budget from the very first conversation — with no commitment required.

02 // COORDINATION

Coordinators

They turn your requirements into a clear action plan, assign tasks to the right specialists, and track every detail to keep the project moving without any delay.

// ABOUT SIMPLECODE

We make web simple, professional, and affordable.

SimpleCode was born from a simple idea: every business deserves a professional web presence without the enterprise price tag. We've stripped away the unnecessary overhead and built a model that delivers high-quality results at a fraction of what other agencies charge.

"Our name says it all — we simplify code, simplify pricing, and simplify the entire process."

Our low-cost approach is powered by a smart use of templates for standard projects. For clients who need something more bespoke, we offer fully custom development from scratch. Either way, you get the same dedication, quality, and attention to detail.
